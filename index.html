Before You Start

What you'll need

A few basics — most of which you already own — are all it takes to keep your Brunelle looking its best.

  • —Sulfate-free shampoo & conditioner
  • —Wide-tooth comb or wig brush
  • —Wig stand for drying and storage
  • —Silk or satin bonnet, or a silk pillowcase
  • —Leave-in conditioner or a light hair serum
  • —Heat protectant spray, for styling days

Step By Step

Washing

Wash every 2–3 weeks, depending on how often the piece is worn and how much product builds up.

1

Detangle first

Work gently from ends to roots with a wide-tooth comb before any water touches the hair.

2

Cleanse gently

Submerge in lukewarm water and work through a small amount of sulfate-free shampoo — never rub, twist or scrub.

3

Rinse thoroughly

Rinse in lukewarm water until it runs completely clear.

4

Condition mid-to-ends

Apply from mid-length to ends, avoiding the lace. Leave for 5–10 minutes, then rinse well.

5

Towel dry, don't wring

Blot gently with a soft towel to lift away excess water.

Finishing Up

Drying & styling

1

Air dry on a stand

Place on a wig stand and let it dry away from direct sunlight.

2

Apply leave-in while damp

A light serum or leave-in keeps the hair soft and moisturized as it dries.

3

Heat, sparingly

Always use a heat protectant first. Keep tools on low-to-medium heat and avoid daily styling.

Know The Rules

Do's & don'ts

Do

  • Wash every 2–3 weeks
  • Store on a wig stand
  • Detangle before every wash
  • Sleep in a satin bonnet
  • Use sulfate-free products

Don't

  • Sleep in your wig
  • Use excessive heat
  • Rub or twist wet hair
  • Lend it out — oils transfer
  • Store it in a bag or drawer
A Note On Storage

When it's not on your head, it should be on a stand — in a cool, dry spot, out of direct sunlight. This is what keeps the shape, the shine and the lifespan intact.
